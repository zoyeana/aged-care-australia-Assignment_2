Reading the data

  • Recipient counts are service-location records, not unique people.
  • Recipient counts are not service capacity or beds.
  • Facility counts do not measure access or unmet demand.
  • Each map dot is a group of five facilities.
  • Dot positions are representative, not exact locations.
  • Groups with fewer than five facilities are not shown.
  • Package percentages are within each age group.
  • Home Care Package levels reflect assessed support needs.
  • Level 1 is basic support. Level 4 is high support.
  • Charts show snapshots, not individuals moving between levels.
